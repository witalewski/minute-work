Information used by the app

Minute Work is a workout timing tool. It does not require an account. The current app does not collect or transmit personal information, workout results, health measurements, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or payment details to the publisher. It does not include advertising or analytics services.

The selected round count and current timer state are processed on your device during the session. The app does not provide a saved workout history or cloud synchronisation.

Device features

The app uses device audio and vibration for timer cues and keeps the display awake while a timer is running. Android declares internet access, but the current app contains no publisher data-upload feature. It does not access Health Connect or Apple Health, record microphone audio, or request location access.
