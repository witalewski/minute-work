Minute Work is a focused every-minute-on-the-minute workout timer.

Start a session

Choose 5, 10, 15 or 20 rounds, then tap Start workout. A three-second countdown comes before the first 60-second round. Follow your qualified professional's prescribed repetitions and rest for the time remaining in each minute. The timer continues during rest.

Pause, resume or end

Pause stops the clock. Resume continues the session. Reset ends it and returns to setup. Go again starts another session after completion.

Sound and vibration

Check device volume, audio output and vibration settings. Vibration requires compatible hardware. Keep the app visible during your session; reliable background or locked-screen cues are not promised. The active timer requests that the display stay awake.

Appearance

The app follows your device's light or dark appearance setting.

Learn about EMOM

Tap What is EMOM? in the header. The guide explains timing and controls. Opening it does not pause a running timer. Minute Work does not prescribe exercises, provide medical advice or assess technique.

Workout history and accounts

No account is required. The current app does not save a workout history or synchronise sessions between devices.
